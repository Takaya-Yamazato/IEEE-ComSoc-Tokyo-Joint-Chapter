# 14th edition of the IEEE ComSoc Student Competition

The Competition rules and details, including the winners of the previous editions, are presented at:http://www.comsoc.org/communications-technology-changing-world. 

The theme of the competition is again "Communications Technology Changing the World”.

Please promote the Student Competition and solicit participation among the Student and Graduate Student Members of your Chapters. 

The deadline is Sept. 19, 2026.

Each Chapter should nominate a Champion to participate!

Please highlight that the monetary prizes have been increased as follows:
- 1st Prize:      USD 3,000. 
- 2nd Prize:      USD 2,000. 
- 3rd Prize:      USD 1,000. 

Moreover, the winner will be invited to receive the Prize at GLOBECOM 2026, Macau, with expenses paid.

Additional rewards will be given to the winners and the recipients of Honorary Mentions, including coupons to attend ComSoc Conferences.
